1. **First pass (≈ 60% of time):** answer everything you can solve in < 60 seconds. Calendar, directions, coding, blood relations, unit digit, divisibility, train/boat formula questions are quick.
2. **Second pass:** the multi-step ones (partnership with joining dates, pipes with closing, election percentages).
3. **Use the options:** plug options back into the question (back-solving) for ratio/age/number problems. It's often faster than algebra.
4. **Approximate:** if the options are far apart, round the numbers (e.g. 63 km/h ≈ 17.5 m/s).
5. **Units check:** answer in m/s or km/h? minutes or hours? Many wrong options are the right number in the wrong unit.
6. **"None of these"** is genuinely correct sometimes in R.S. Aggarwal-style questions. If your answer is clean and not listed, trust it.
7. **Never leave a question blank** unless there's negative marking (check the paper's instructions).

**You've got this. Read a topic, do its MCQs, then move to the next.**
