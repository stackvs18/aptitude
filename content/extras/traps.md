1. **"None of these"** is correct surprisingly often in book questions (Average Q4, Q13; SI Q7; Partnership Q1; Trains Q7; Work Q11). Don't force-fit.
2. Gain/Loss % is always on **C.P.**, never on S.P.
3. Repair/transport costs are added to **C.P.**
4. Train crossing a **pole/man** → distance = train length only; crossing a **platform/bridge/train** → add lengths.
5. km/h → m/s: **× 5/18**; m/s → km/h: **× 18/5**.
6. Same distance at two speeds → average speed is **2xy/(x + y)**, not (x + y)/2.
7. Bells tolling together: count = (time/LCM) **+ 1**.
8. Leap-day rule: the extra odd day counts only if **29 Feb lies inside** the interval (see Calendar Q4).
9. HCF "same remainder" → HCF of **differences**; HCF "remainders r₁, r₂" → HCF of (N₁ − r₁, N₂ − r₂).
10. Partnership with different times → multiply **capital × months**.
