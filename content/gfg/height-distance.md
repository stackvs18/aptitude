## Trigonometric ratios (right triangle)
For angle θ: **sin = perpendicular/hypotenuse, cos = base/hypotenuse, tan = perpendicular/base.**
cosec = 1/sin, sec = 1/cos, cot = 1/tan. Identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

## Standard values
| θ | 0° | 30° | 45° | 60° | 90° |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sin | 0 | 1/2 | 1/√2 | √3/2 | 1 |
| cos | 1 | √3/2 | 1/√2 | 1/2 | 0 |
| tan | 0 | 1/√3 | 1 | √3 | ∞ |

## Core concepts
- **Line of sight:** from the eye to the object.
- **Angle of elevation:** looking **up** from the horizontal.
- **Angle of depression:** looking **down** from the horizontal. The angle of depression from A to B equals the angle of elevation from B to A.
- Draw the right triangle; tan is used most because it links height and horizontal distance.

## Applications and examples
- From 30 m away, the top of a tower is at 60° elevation → height = 30 × tan 60° = **30√3 ≈ 51.96 m**.
- Shadow length equals height when the sun's elevation is **45°**.
- From a 100 m cliff, a boat is seen at 30° depression → distance = 100/tan 30° = **100√3 m**.
