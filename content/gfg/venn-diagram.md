## What it is
Venn diagrams show relationships between groups as circles. Questions ask which diagram fits a set of categories, or ask you to count regions.

## Basic relationships
| Relation | Diagram | Example |
|---|---|---|
| One inside another | circle within a circle | Rose ⊂ Flower |
| Chain of three inside each other | three concentric circles | Kolkata ⊂ West Bengal ⊂ India |
| Two separate inside a third | two separate circles in a big one | Doctors, Lawyers ⊂ Professionals |
| Partial overlap | overlapping circles | Women, Doctors, Mothers |
| No relation | separate circles | Pens, Tables, Rivers |
| One inside, one overlapping | one circle inside, one cutting across | Animals ⊃ Dogs; Pets overlaps both |

## Geometrical (region counting)
A figure of a triangle, circle and square overlapping, with numbers in each region. Read questions like "numbers in the circle and the square but not the triangle" region by region.

## Set formula
n(A ∪ B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A ∩ B).
*Example:* of 50 students, 30 like tea, 25 like coffee and 10 like both → like at least one = 30 + 25 − 10 = **45**, neither = **5**.
