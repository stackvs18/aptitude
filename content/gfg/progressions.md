## Arithmetic progression (AP)
Each term = previous + common difference d. (3, 7, 11, 15 …, d = 4)
- **nth term: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d.**
- **Sum: Sₙ = n/2 [2a + (n − 1)d] = n/2 (first + last).**
- Three numbers in AP: take a − d, a, a + d. Arithmetic mean of a and b = (a + b)/2.

## Geometric progression (GP)
Each term = previous × common ratio r. (2, 6, 18, 54 …, r = 3)
- **nth term: aₙ = a·rⁿ⁻¹.**
- **Sum: Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1)** (r ≠ 1). **Infinite sum** (|r| < 1) = **a/(1 − r)**.
- Geometric mean of a and b = √(ab).

## Harmonic progression (HP)
Numbers whose **reciprocals** are in AP (1, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4 …). Harmonic mean of a and b = 2ab/(a + b) (the same formula as average speed).
For positive numbers: **AM ≥ GM ≥ HM**, and GM² = AM × HM.

## Solved examples
- Sum of the first 20 odd numbers = 20² = **400**.
- 10th term of 5, 8, 11 … = 5 + 9 × 3 = **32**.
- Sum of 1 + ½ + ¼ + … = 1/(1 − ½) = **2**.
