## What it is
Read a passage and answer questions about it.

## Question types
- **Main idea/title:** what the whole passage is about.
- **Specific detail:** facts stated directly.
- **Inference:** what the passage implies but doesn't state.
- **Vocabulary in context:** the meaning of a word as used in the passage.
- **Tone/attitude:** the author's tone (critical, neutral, optimistic …).
- **Author's purpose:** to inform, persuade, criticise or describe.

## Approach
1. Read the questions first, then the passage with them in mind.
2. Note the main idea of each paragraph in a few words.
3. Answer only from the passage, not from your own knowledge.
4. For vocabulary questions, substitute each option into the sentence.
5. Eliminate options that are too extreme or out of scope.
