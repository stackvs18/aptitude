## What it is
Find the correctly (or wrongly) spelled word among the options.

## Commonly misspelled words
accommodate, achieve, acquire, apparent, beginning, believe, calendar, committee, conscience, conscious, definitely, embarrass, environment, existence, February, guarantee, harass, immediately, independent, maintenance, millennium, necessary, occasion, occurrence, privilege, receive, recommend, rhythm, separate, successful, tomorrow, until, vacuum, Wednesday.

## Spelling rules
- **i before e except after c**: believe, receive (exceptions: weird, seize, neither).
- Doubling: begin → beginning; occur → occurred (stress on the last syllable).
- Drop silent e before a vowel suffix: make → making; keep it before a consonant: hope → hopeful.
- y → i before most suffixes: happy → happiness; but keep y before -ing: study → studying.

## Tips
Split long words into syllables (ac-com-mo-date) and watch double letters.
