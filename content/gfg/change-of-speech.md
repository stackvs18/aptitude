## Types of speech
1. **Direct speech:** the exact words in quotation marks. He said, "I am busy."
2. **Indirect (reported) speech:** the meaning reported without quotes. He said that he was busy.

## Rules
**Tense goes one step back** when the reporting verb is in the past:
| Direct | Indirect |
|---|---|
| present simple (am/is) | past simple (was) |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| present perfect | past perfect |
| past simple | past perfect |
| will / can / may | would / could / might |

**No change** when the reporting verb is in the present ("He says…"), or the statement is a universal truth ("The sun rises in the east").

**Time and place words change:** now → then; today → that day; tomorrow → the next day; yesterday → the previous day; here → there; this → that; ago → before.

**Pronouns change** to match the speaker's viewpoint ("I" → he/she).

## Sentence types
- **Questions:** said → asked; yes/no questions use "if/whether"; question order becomes a statement. He said, "Are you coming?" → He asked if I was coming.
- **Commands/requests:** said → told/ordered/requested + **to** + verb. He said, "Sit down." → He told me to sit down.
- **Exclamations:** said → exclaimed with joy/sorrow. "What a goal!" → He exclaimed that it was a great goal.
