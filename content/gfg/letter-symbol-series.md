## What it is
An **alphanumeric series** mixes letters, numbers and symbols. Questions ask about positions ("the 5th element to the right of the 12th from the left") or patterns.

## Common types
1. **Letter–number alternating:** A1, C3, E5, G7 → **I9** (letters +2, numbers +2).
2. **Letter–number embedded:** the number is the letter's position: B2, D4, F6 → **H8**.
3. **Position-based** (A = 1 … Z = 26): convert letters to numbers and look for the rule.
4. **Mixed operations:** letters shift by one rule and numbers by another (A2, C6, E18 → letters +2, numbers ×3 → **G54**).
5. **Reverse alphabet:** Z = 1, Y = 2 … or letters running backwards (Z, X, V, T → **R**).

## Arrangement questions
For a long row like "R 4 # K 7 @ M 2 …":
- "Xth from the left" and "Yth from the right": count carefully; **position from right = total − position from left + 1**.
- "Which letters are immediately followed by a number?": scan pair by pair.
- "How many symbols are between a letter and a number?": underline each match as you go.

## Tips
- Memorise positions with **EJOTY** (E 5, J 10, O 15, T 20, Y 25).
- Opposite letter pairs add to 27: A-Z, B-Y, C-X …
- Mark the row on paper; never count in your head.
