## Types of patterns
1. **Alphabetical order:** skip a fixed number. A, C, E, G → **I** (+2).
2. **Position-based:** use A = 1 … Z = 26. B, E, H, K → 2, 5, 8, 11 → **N** (14).
3. **Reverse alphabet:** Z, X, V, T → **R**.
4. **Repetition and alternation:** AB, BC, CD …; two interleaved series (A, Z, B, Y, C → **X**).
5. **Increasing gaps:** A, B, D, G, K → +1, +2, +3, +4 → **P**.
6. **Missing-letter blocks:** "ab_ba_ab_" style. Find the repeating unit.

## Tips
- Write the positions under the letters.
- Use EJOTY to find positions fast.
- After Z, wrap back to A (Y + 3 = B).
