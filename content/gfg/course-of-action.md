## What it is
A statement describes a problem or situation. Decide which suggested **courses of action** logically follow, i.e. are practical steps that solve or reduce the problem.

## What makes a course of action valid
- It addresses the problem directly.
- It is **practical** and **reasonable** in the real world.
- It improves the situation without causing a bigger problem.
- Extreme actions (ban everything, shut down, punish everyone) usually don't follow.

## Example
Statement: Many people in a town fell ill after drinking contaminated water.
- I. The water supply authority should check and clean the supply. → **Follows.**
- II. People should stop drinking water. → **Doesn't follow** (impractical).

## Steps
1. Find the actual problem in the statement.
2. Check each action: does it help, is it practical, and is it proportionate?
3. Choose "only I", "only II", "either", "neither" or "both" accordingly.
