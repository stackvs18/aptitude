## Formula
**Average (arithmetic mean) = Sum of observations ÷ Number of observations.** So Sum = Average × Number.
*Example:* average of 4, 8, 12, 16 = 40/4 = **10**.

## Mean vs average
"Average" in aptitude means the arithmetic mean. Mean, median and mode are all measures of central tendency, but MCQs almost always want the mean.

## Negative numbers and two numbers
- Negatives are added with their signs: average of −5, 3, 8 = 6/3 = 2.
- Average of two numbers a and b = (a + b)/2.

## Important formulas
- First n natural numbers: (n + 1)/2. Squares: (n + 1)(2n + 1)/6. Cubes: n(n + 1)²/4.
- First n even numbers: n + 1. First n odd numbers: n.
- Consecutive numbers (or any arithmetic progression): average = (first + last)/2 = middle term.
- **Average speed** for equal distances at x and y = **2xy/(x + y)** (not (x + y)/2).
- **Weighted average** = (n₁a₁ + n₂a₂)/(n₁ + n₂).
- If every value increases by k, the average increases by k; if every value is multiplied by k, so is the average.

## Replacement and new-entry shortcuts
- A new person replaces one of weight w and the average of n people rises by d → new person = **w + n·d**.
- A newcomer joins n people and the average rises by d → newcomer = **new average + n·d**.
- A batsman's average rises by d after scoring R in innings n + 1 → new average = **R − n·d**.

## Solved example
The average of 11 results is 60. The first 6 average 58 and the last 6 average 63. The 6th result = 6 × 58 + 6 × 63 − 11 × 60 = **66** (it was counted twice).
