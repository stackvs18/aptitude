## The rules, 1 to 20
| Number | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | every number | — |
| 2 | last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8 | 3,458 ✔ |
| 3 | sum of digits divisible by 3 | 1,527 → 15 ✔ |
| 4 | last two digits divisible by 4 (or end in 00) | 7,316 → 16 ✔ |
| 5 | last digit 0 or 5 | 985 ✔ |
| 6 | divisible by both 2 and 3 | 1,632 ✔ |
| 7 | double the last digit, subtract it from the rest; repeat until small | 343 → 34 − 6 = 28 ✔ |
| 8 | last three digits divisible by 8 (or end in 000) | 5,112 → 112 ✔ |
| 9 | sum of digits divisible by 9 | 7,281 → 18 ✔ |
| 10 | last digit 0 | 340 ✔ |
| 11 | (sum of digits at odd places) − (sum at even places) = 0 or a multiple of 11 | 2,728 → (8 + 7) − (2 + 2) = 11 ✔ |
| 12 | divisible by 3 and 4 | 1,644 ✔ |
| 13 | multiply the last digit by 4 and **add** it to the rest; repeat | 169 → 16 + 36 = 52 = 4 × 13 ✔ |
| 14 | divisible by 2 and 7 | 210 ✔ |
| 15 | divisible by 3 and 5 | 1,035 ✔ |
| 16 | last four digits divisible by 16 | 12,032 → 2,032 = 16 × 127 ✔ |
| 17 | multiply the last digit by 5 and **subtract** it from the rest; repeat | 221 → 22 − 5 = 17 ✔ |
| 18 | divisible by 2 and 9 | 1,458 ✔ |
| 19 | multiply the last digit by 2 and **add** it to the rest; repeat | 361 → 36 + 2 = 38 ✔ |
| 20 | last two digits divisible by 20 (ends in 00, 20, 40, 60, 80) | 3,460 ✔ |

## How to use them in MCQs
- For a composite divisor, split it into **co-prime** factors and test both: 24 = 3 × 8 (not 4 × 6), 36 = 4 × 9, 45 = 5 × 9, 72 = 8 × 9.
- **Missing digit** questions: write the rule as an equation. *Example:* 78*3945 divisible by 11 → (5 + 9 + * + 7) − (4 + 3 + 8) = 6 + * must be 0 or 11 → * = 5.
- 1001 = 7 × 11 × 13, so a 6-digit number like abcabc is always divisible by 7, 11 and 13.
