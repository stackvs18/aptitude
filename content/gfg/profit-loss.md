## Basic terms
- **Cost Price (CP):** what the seller paid. **Selling Price (SP):** what it's sold for.
- **Marked Price (MP)/list price:** printed price before discount.
- **Profit** = SP − CP (when SP > CP). **Loss** = CP − SP (when CP > SP).
- **Overheads** (transport, repairs) are added to CP.

## Formulas
| Find | Formula |
|---|---|
| Profit % | Profit/CP × 100 |
| Loss % | Loss/CP × 100 |
| SP from profit % | CP × (100 + P%)/100 |
| SP from loss % | CP × (100 − L%)/100 |
| CP from SP and profit % | SP × 100/(100 + P%) |
| CP from SP and loss % | SP × 100/(100 − L%) |
| Discount | MP − SP; Discount % = Discount/MP × 100 |

**Profit and loss % are always on CP. Discount % is always on MP.**

## Relationship between SP and CP
- SP > CP → profit; SP < CP → loss; SP = CP → neither.
- **CP of x items = SP of y items** → profit/loss % = (x − y)/y × 100 (profit if x > y).
  *Example:* CP of 10 = SP of 7 → 3/7 × 100 = **42.86% profit**.

## Shortcut methods
- Two items sold at the **same SP**, one at x% profit and one at x% loss → **always a loss of x²/100 %**.
- **False weight** (sells at CP but gives less): gain % = error/(true value − error) × 100. Using 900 g for 1 kg → 100/900 × 100 = **11.11%**.
- **Successive discounts** d₁ and d₂ → single discount = d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100.
- **Mark-up m% then discount d%** → profit % = m − d − md/100.
- Profit at price S₁ equals loss at price S₂ → **CP = (S₁ + S₂)/2**.

## Properties and applications
Profit % and loss % let you compare deals of different sizes. They're used in shop pricing, discounts and trading questions.
