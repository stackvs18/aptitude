## Types of voice
- **Active:** the subject does the action. "Ram **writes** a letter."
- **Passive:** the subject receives the action. "A letter **is written** by Ram."

## Active → passive
1. Make the object the new subject.
2. Use the correct form of **be** + **past participle (V3)**.
3. Put the old subject after "by" (often dropped).

| Tense | Active | Passive |
|---|---|---|
| Simple present | writes | is/am/are written |
| Present continuous | is writing | is being written |
| Present perfect | has written | has been written |
| Simple past | wrote | was/were written |
| Past continuous | was writing | was being written |
| Past perfect | had written | had been written |
| Simple future | will write | will be written |
| Future perfect | will have written | will have been written |
| Modals | can write | can be written |

(Perfect continuous tenses and future continuous are not changed into the passive.)

## Special cases
- **Imperative:** "Shut the door." → "**Let** the door be shut."
- **Questions:** "Did he finish the work?" → "**Was** the work finished by him?"
- Pronouns change case: I → me, he → him, they → them.
- Intransitive verbs (no object) have no passive.

## Passive → active
Reverse the steps: the "by" phrase becomes the subject, and the verb returns to the active form of the same tense.
