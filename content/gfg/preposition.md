## What it is
A word that shows the relation of a noun or pronoun to another word (time, place, direction): in, on, at, by, with, for, to, from.

## Types
- **Time:** at 5 pm, on Monday, in July / in 2026, since 2020 (a point in time), for two hours (a duration).
- **Place:** at the station (a point), on the table (a surface), in the room (inside).
- **Direction/movement:** to, into, onto, towards, through.
- **Agent/instrument:** by (written by), with (cut with a knife).

## Confusing pairs
| Pair | Rule |
|---|---|
| In / into | in = position; into = movement inside |
| On / upon | similar; "upon" is more formal |
| Beside / besides | beside = next to; besides = in addition to |
| Between / among | between two; among three or more |
| Since / for | since + point in time; for + duration |
| By / with | by = agent; with = tool |

## Fixed prepositional phrases
Agree **with** a person, agree **to** a proposal; angry **with** a person, angry **at** a thing; afraid **of**; good **at**; interested **in**; married **to**; senior **to**; superior **to**; prefer A **to** B; depend **on**; congratulate **on**; deal **with**; comply **with**; abide **by**.

## Tips
Many errors come from fixed phrases. Memorise the verb–preposition and adjective–preposition pairs above.
