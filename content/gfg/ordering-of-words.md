## What it is
Words or phrases are jumbled. Arrange them into a meaningful, grammatical sentence.

## Basic word order
English follows **Subject – Verb – Object (SVO)**: "The boy (S) kicked (V) the ball (O)."
Extended: Subject + Verb + Object + Manner + Place + Time → "She sang beautifully at the concert yesterday."

## Structures
- Questions: auxiliary + subject + verb ("Did you finish?").
- Adjective order: **opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose** + noun ("a beautiful small old round red Indian wooden dining table").
- Adverbs of frequency come before the main verb (always, often, never) but after "be" ("He is always late").

## Tips
1. Find the subject and the main verb first.
2. Attach articles and adjectives to their nouns.
3. Place time and place phrases at the end.
4. Use the options: eliminate any that start or end impossibly.
