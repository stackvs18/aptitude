## What it is
Find the **central idea** (the main message) of a passage, story or poem, as opposed to its topic or details.

## Examples of themes
Courage, love and sacrifice, good vs evil, the cost of ambition, coming of age, man vs nature.

## Steps to detect the theme
1. Identify the subject (what the passage is about).
2. Notice what happens or changes, and the author's attitude.
3. Look for repeated ideas, words or images.
4. Put the message in one sentence that applies beyond this passage.

## Example
A passage about a student who fails repeatedly but keeps practising and finally succeeds → theme: **perseverance leads to success** (not "exams" or "a student").

## Tip
The right option is broad enough to cover the whole passage, not just one paragraph.
