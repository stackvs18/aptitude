## What it is
A word that describes or qualifies a noun or pronoun ("a **tall** boy", "She is **clever**").

## Types
- **Descriptive/quality:** brave, red, happy.
- **Quantity:** some, much, little, enough (uncountable).
- **Number:** one, first, few, many, several (countable).
- **Demonstrative:** this, that, these, those.
- **Possessive:** my, your, his, her, their.
- **Interrogative:** which, what, whose.
- **Distributive:** each, every, either, neither.
- **Proper:** made from proper nouns (Indian, French).

## Degrees of comparison
| Positive | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| tall | taller | tallest |
| beautiful | more beautiful | most beautiful |
| good | better | best |
| bad | worse | worst |
| little | less | least |
| many/much | more | most |

## Rules
- Don't double comparatives: "more better" ✗.
- **Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, prefer** take **to**, not "than".
- **Few** (countable) vs **little** (uncountable); "a few" or "a little" = some, while "few" or "little" alone = hardly any.
- **Elder/eldest** for family members; **older/oldest** for people and things in general.
- Order of adjectives: opinion, size, age, shape, colour, origin, material, purpose.
- Comparing two people or things → comparative ("the **taller** of the two").
