## Percentage formula
**Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100.** "x% of y" = xy/100.
*Example:* 45 out of 60 = 45/60 × 100 = **75%**.

## How to calculate quickly
- 10% = move the decimal one place; 5% = half of 10%; 1% = divide by 100.
- 15% of 80 = 10% (8) + 5% (4) = **12**.
- x% of y = y% of x (16% of 25 = 25% of 16 = 4).

## Percentage change and difference
- **% change = (new − old)/old × 100** (increase if positive).
- **Percentage difference** between two values a and b = |a − b| ÷ ((a + b)/2) × 100.
- **Percentage point** = simple difference between two percentages (12% → 15% is 3 percentage points but a 25% increase).

## Tricks
- A is r% more than B → B is **r/(100 + r) × 100%** less than A. (25% more → 20% less.)
- A is r% less than B → B is **r/(100 − r) × 100%** more than A.
- Price up r% → consumption must fall r/(100 + r) × 100% to keep spending fixed.
- **Successive changes** a% and b% → net = a + b + ab/100 (use −b for a decrease). +10% then −10% = −1%.
- Population/value after n years at r% p.a. = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ (or (1 − r/100)ⁿ for depreciation).
