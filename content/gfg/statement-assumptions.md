## What it is
An **assumption** is something taken for granted (unstated) by the person making a statement. Decide which given assumptions are **implicit**.

## How to test an assumption
- **Negation test:** reverse the assumption. If the statement no longer makes sense, the assumption is implicit.
- It is never written in the statement itself; it supports the statement.
- Extreme words ("only", "all", "best") usually make an assumption invalid.

## Example
Statement: "Use brand X soap for soft skin," says an advertisement.
- I. People want soft skin. → **Implicit** (otherwise, why advertise it?).
- II. Brand X is the only soap for soft skin. → **Not implicit** ("only" is too strong).

## Tips
- Think from the speaker's viewpoint: what must they believe to say this?
- Advertisements assume people read ads and are influenced by them.
- Appeals and notices assume people will respond to them.
