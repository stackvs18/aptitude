## Terms
- **Random experiment:** an action with uncertain outcomes (tossing a coin).
- **Outcome:** one possible result. **Sample space (S):** all outcomes.
- **Event (E):** a set of outcomes we care about. **Favourable outcomes:** outcomes in E.

## Formula and range
**P(E) = favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes**, and **0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1**. Impossible event → 0, certain event → 1.

## Basic rules
- **Complement:** P(not E) = 1 − P(E).
- **Addition:** P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B). If mutually exclusive: P(A) + P(B).
- **Multiplication (independent):** P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B).

## Standard sample spaces
- One coin: 2; two coins: 4; n coins: 2ⁿ.
- One die: 6; two dice: 36. Most likely sum of two dice = **7** (6 ways).
- A pack of 52 cards: 4 suits of 13; 26 red and 26 black; 12 face cards (J, Q, K); 4 aces.

## Examples
- P(head on one toss) = 1/2. P(at least one head in 2 tosses) = 1 − 1/4 = **3/4**.
- P(a king from a pack) = 4/52 = **1/13**.
- P(sum 8 with two dice) = 5/36.
