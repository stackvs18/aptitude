## What it is
A part of a sentence is underlined. Choose the option that improves it, or "no improvement".

## Key areas
- Tense and sequence of tenses.
- Subject–verb agreement.
- Correct preposition, article and pronoun.
- Idiomatic usage ("cope **with**", "comply **with**").
- Word choice and conciseness.
- Conditional sentences: "If I **had known**, I **would have come**."

## Example
"He **did not knew** the answer." → **did not know** (after "did", use the base verb).

## Tips
- Replace the underlined part with each option and read the full sentence.
- Pick "no improvement" only when the original is clearly correct.
- Prefer the shortest option that is grammatically correct and keeps the meaning.
