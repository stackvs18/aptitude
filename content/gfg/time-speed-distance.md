## Units
- Distance: m, km. Time: s, h. Speed: m/s, km/h.
- **km/h → m/s: × 5/18. m/s → km/h: × 18/5.** (36 km/h = 10 m/s; 72 km/h = 20 m/s.)

## The relationship
**Distance = Speed × Time** (Speed = D/T, Time = D/S).
- Distance fixed → speed and time are **inversely** proportional (speeds a : b → times b : a).
- Time fixed → distance ∝ speed. Speed fixed → distance ∝ time.

## Key formulas
- **Average speed** = total distance ÷ total time. Equal distances at x and y → **2xy/(x + y)**.
- **Relative speed:** opposite directions → **add**; same direction → **subtract**.
- **Meeting time** (towards each other from distance D) = D/(S₁ + S₂). **Catch-up time** = gap/(S₁ − S₂).
- At a/b of usual speed, a person is t minutes late → usual time = t × a/(b − a). (At ¾ speed 20 min late → 20 × 3/1 = 60 min.)
- Late at speed S₁ and early at S₂ (total gap t) → distance = S₁S₂t/(S₂ − S₁).
- After meeting, A and B take t₁ and t₂ to finish → S_A : S_B = √t₂ : √t₁.

## Applications
Trains, boats, races, circular tracks and clocks are all special cases of D = S × T.
