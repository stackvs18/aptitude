## What it is
A passage with several blanks. Fill each with the word that fits grammar, meaning and context.

## Question types
- Grammar-based blanks (tense, article, preposition, conjunction).
- Vocabulary-based blanks (choosing the word with the right shade of meaning).
- Fixed blanks with multiple options per blank, or a word bank shared by all blanks.

## Common word mistakes
affect/effect, then/than, its/it's, their/there/they're, lose/loose, advice/advise, practice/practise, among/between (three or more vs two).

## Tips and tricks
1. Read the whole passage once to get the theme and tone.
2. Fill the easiest blanks first.
3. Check the words just before and after each blank (collocations, prepositions).
4. Re-read the passage with your answers to check the flow.
