## What it is
Choose the correct form of a sentence (or the part that needs correcting).

## Rules
1. **Subject–verb agreement:** "The list of items **is** on the desk." "Either the teacher or the students **are** responsible" (verb agrees with the nearer subject).
2. **Repetition/redundancy:** remove doubled words: "return back", "free gift", "past history".
3. **Each of / one of / none of** + plural noun + singular verb: "Each of the students **has** a book."
4. **Present continuous:** stative verbs (know, believe, love, understand) are not used in -ing form: "I **know**", not "I am knowing".
5. **Parallelism:** keep listed items in the same form: "He likes **to run, to swim and to cycle**."
6. **Modifiers:** place a modifier next to what it describes. "Walking down the street, **I saw** the trees" (not "the trees were seen by me").
7. **Comparisons:** compare like with like: "His salary is higher than **that of** his brother."

## Tip
Find the error first, then pick the option that fixes it without adding a new error.
