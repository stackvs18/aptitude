## What it is
A list of numbers that follows a rule. Find the rule, then the next or missing term (or the wrong term).

## Types of number series
- **Arithmetic:** constant difference. 3, 8, 13, 18 → +5 → **23**.
- **Geometric:** constant ratio. 2, 6, 18, 54 → ×3 → **162**.
- **Difference of differences:** 2, 6, 12, 20, 30 → differences 4, 6, 8, 10 → **42**.
- **Squares/cubes:** 1, 4, 9, 16 (n²); 1, 8, 27, 64 (n³); n² ± 1, n³ ± 1.
- **Mixed operations:** ×2 + 1, ×3 − 1 … 3, 7, 15, 31 → ×2 + 1 → **63**.
- **Alternating series:** two series interleaved. 4, 6, 12, 14, 28, 30 → +2, ×2 → **60**.
- **Prime series:** 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 …
- **Fibonacci-type:** each term = sum of the previous two. 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 → **13**.

## Order to check patterns
1. Differences (and differences of those).
2. Ratios.
3. Squares, cubes and primes nearby.
4. Alternate terms.
5. A combination like ×n + k.

## Tips
- A sharp rise usually means multiplication or powers; a slow rise means addition.
- In "find the wrong number" questions, the odd term breaks the pattern found from the other terms.
