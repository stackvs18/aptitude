## What it is
Similar to para jumbles: arrange given sentences to form a coherent paragraph, or choose the sentence that best completes or starts a paragraph.

## Tips and tricks
- Identify the **topic sentence**: the main idea, usually first.
- Each following sentence should develop, explain or give an example of the one before.
- Use **transition words** to link ideas: firstly, furthermore, in addition, however, consequently, in conclusion.
- Keep the **tense and point of view** consistent.
- The last sentence should conclude or summarise.
