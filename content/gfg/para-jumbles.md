## What it is
Sentences of a paragraph are shuffled. Arrange them in the correct logical order.

## Types
- **Type 1:** all sentences are jumbled.
- **Type 2:** the first and/or last sentence is fixed; arrange the middle.
- Variations: find the odd sentence out, or the sentence that doesn't fit.

## How to solve
1. **Find the opening sentence:** it introduces the topic, and has no pronoun (he, it, this) or connector (however, therefore) pointing backwards.
2. **Find mandatory pairs:** a sentence that introduces a name followed by one using "he"; a general statement followed by an example; an acronym's full form before the short form.
3. **Follow connectors:** "however" shows contrast with the previous sentence; "also" or "moreover" add to it; "thus" or "therefore" conclude.
4. **Find the closing sentence:** a summary or conclusion.
5. Use the options to check your pairs quickly.

## Tips
- Chronology: dates and time words (first, then, finally).
- Articles: "a" introduces something new; "the" refers to something already mentioned.
