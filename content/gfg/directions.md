## Things to remember
- N–E, E–S, S–W and W–N are 90° apart. N–NE, NE–E and so on are 45° apart.
- Clockwise order: N → E → S → W. Anticlockwise: N → W → S → E.
- **Right turn = 90° clockwise; left turn = 90° anticlockwise.** x° right = x° clockwise.
- Right of N = E, of E = S, of S = W, of W = N. Left of N = W, of W = S, of S = E, of E = N.
- Right of NE = SE; right of SE = SW; left of NE = NW; left of SE = NE.

## Steps
1. Draw the path on a 2D grid (N up, E right) from the starting point.
2. Track the direction you're **facing** after every turn.
3. Shortest distance = √(x² + y²) (Pythagoras: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 6-8-10).
4. Direction from the start: compare the final x and y (e.g. +3, +4 → north-east).

## Question types
a) **Basic:** walk 10 m north, turn right 5 m, turn right 10 m → **5 m east** of the start.
b) **Degree-based:** facing north, turn 90° clockwise, then 180° anticlockwise, then 270° clockwise → net +180° → **south**.
c) **Coded:** "P # Q means P is north of Q" → decode each symbol, then draw.

## Sun and shadows
Morning: sun in the east, shadows fall west. Evening: sun in the west, shadows fall east.
In the morning, a shadow on your right means you face **south**; on your left, you face **north**.
