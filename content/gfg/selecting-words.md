## What it is
Choose the most suitable word to fill a blank so the sentence is correct in meaning and grammar.

## Tips and tricks
- Read the full sentence and decide the meaning **before** looking at the options.
- Check **grammar**: part of speech, tense, singular/plural, article (a/an).
- Check **collocations**: "commit a crime", "make a decision", "pay attention", "heavy rain".
- Watch **confusable words**: affect/effect, accept/except, principal/principle, stationary/stationery, complement/compliment, loose/lose.
- Contrast words (but, although, however) signal an opposite meaning; "and" or "also" signal a similar meaning.

## Example
"The teacher's _____ explanation helped everyone understand." (a) lucid (b) obscure (c) vague → **lucid**.
