## Fundamentals
- **Inlet** fills the tank (positive work). **Outlet/leak** empties it (negative work).
- A pipe fills in n hours → fills 1/n per hour. An outlet empties in m hours → −1/m per hour.

## Formulas
- Two inlets a and b together: **ab/(a + b)**.
- Inlet a and outlet b (b > a): tank fills in **ab/(b − a)**.
- Inlet fills in a hours but takes t with a leak → leak alone empties in **at/(t − a)**.
- Several pipes: 1/T = Σ(1/inlet) − Σ(1/outlet).

## Shortcut: capacity as LCM
Take tank capacity = LCM of the times. Inlet rate +, outlet rate −, then add.
*Example:* A fills in 20 h, B in 30 h, C empties in 40 h. Capacity 120: +6 + 4 − 3 = 7/h → **120/7 = 17 1/7 h**.

## Example
A tap fills in 8 h; a leak empties it in 12 h → net 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24 → **24 h**.
