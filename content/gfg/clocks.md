## Important points
- The dial has **60 minute spaces**. The minute hand moves **6° per minute**; the hour hand **0.5° per minute** (30° per hour).
- The minute hand gains **55 minute spaces per hour** (5.5° per minute) on the hour hand.
- The hands **coincide once every hour** (11 times in 12 hours, 22 times a day).
- They are **opposite** (180°, 30 spaces apart) 11 times in 12 hours, 22 times a day.
- They are at **right angles** (90°, 15 spaces apart) twice every hour: 22 times in 12 hours, 44 times a day.

## Formulas
- **Angle at H:M = |30H − 5.5M|.** If above 180°, subtract from 360°.
  *Example:* 3:40 → |90 − 220| = **130°**.
- Hands together between H and H + 1 at **(60/11) × H** minutes past H. Between 4 and 5: 240/11 = **21 9/11 min past 4**.
- Hands at θ° between H and H + 1: M = (2/11)(30H ± θ).

## Fast and slow clocks
- The hands coincide every 65 5/11 minutes on a correct clock. If they coincide every m minutes, the clock gains (if m < 65 5/11) or loses time.
- A clock that is ahead is "fast", one that is behind is "slow".
- **Mirror image:** actual time = 11:60 − shown time (for 1:00–11:00). 4:20 in a mirror shows 7:40.
