## Trains
- A train crossing a **pole, person or tree** covers its own length: t = L/S.
- Crossing a **platform, bridge or tunnel**: t = (L + P)/S.
- Two trains in **opposite** directions: t = (L₁ + L₂)/(S₁ + S₂). **Same** direction: t = (L₁ + L₂)/(S₁ − S₂).
- Always convert km/h to m/s (× 5/18) when lengths are in metres.
*Example:* 280 m at 63 km/h (17.5 m/s) passes a tree in 280/17.5 = **16 s**.

## Boats and streams: terms
- **Still water:** speed of the boat by itself (u). **Stream:** speed of the current (v).
- **Downstream** = with the current: speed **u + v**.
- **Upstream** = against the current: speed **u − v**.

## Upstream and downstream formulas
- u = (downstream + upstream)/2; v = (downstream − upstream)/2.
- Going and returning a distance d takes total time T → **d = T(u² − v²)/(2u)**.
- Upstream takes k times as long as downstream → **u : v = (k + 1) : (k − 1)**.
- Average speed for a round trip = (u + v)(u − v)/u.

## Solved examples
- Downstream 11 km/h, upstream 5 km/h → boat **8**, stream **3**.
- A man rows 5 km/h in still water; current 1 km/h; round trip takes 1 h → d = 1 × (25 − 1)/10 = **2.4 km**.
