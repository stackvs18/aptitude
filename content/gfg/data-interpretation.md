## What it is
Reading data from a table or chart and answering calculation questions on it (totals, averages, percentages, ratios, growth).

## Types
1. **Tabular:** data in rows and columns. Read the headings and units first.
2. **Bar graph:** compare bar heights; watch the scale on the axis.
3. **Pie chart:** the whole circle is 100% or 360°. 1% = 3.6°. Value = (angle/360) × total.
4. **Line graph:** trends over time. The slope shows the rate of change.
5. **Caselet:** data given as a paragraph. Convert it into a small table first.

## Approach
- Read every question in the set, then calculate only what's asked.
- **% change** = (new − old)/old × 100. **Ratio** questions: simplify early.
- Approximate when the options are far apart.
- Use the fraction table (1/8 = 12.5% etc.) to avoid long division.
