## 2D geometry facts
- **Lines and angles:** angles on a straight line add to 180°; vertically opposite angles are equal; with parallel lines, alternate angles are equal and co-interior angles add to 180°.
- **Triangle:** angles add to 180°; exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles; the sum of any two sides > the third side.
- **Pythagoras:** in a right triangle, hypotenuse² = base² + height². Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.
- **Polygon:** sum of interior angles = (n − 2) × 180°; each exterior angle of a regular polygon = 360°/n; diagonals = n(n − 3)/2.
- **Circle:** the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference; the angle in a semicircle is 90°; a tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact; equal chords are equidistant from the centre.
- **Similar triangles:** corresponding sides are in proportion; areas are in the ratio of the squares of the sides.

## Coordinate geometry
- Distance = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]. Midpoint = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2).
- Slope m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁); line y = mx + c. Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular slopes multiply to −1.

## 3D geometry
Area and volume formulas for cubes, cuboids, cylinders, cones and spheres are in **Mensuration 3D**.

## Solved example
The interior angles of a regular hexagon: (6 − 2) × 180 = 720° in total, so each is **120°**.
