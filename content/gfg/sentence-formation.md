## Types of sentences
1. **Simple:** one independent clause. "She reads."
2. **Compound:** two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction (for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so → **FANBOYS**). "She reads, and he writes."
3. **Complex:** one independent and one or more dependent clauses (because, although, when, if, which). "She reads because she loves stories."
4. **Compound-complex:** two independent clauses plus at least one dependent clause. "She reads because she loves stories, and he writes poems."

## Tips
- Every sentence needs a subject and a verb.
- Join two independent clauses with a conjunction or a semicolon, not just a comma (comma splice).
- Keep the parts parallel: "She likes reading, writing and **swimming**."

## Solved example
Combine "He was tired. He kept working." → "**Although he was tired, he kept working.**" (complex sentence)
