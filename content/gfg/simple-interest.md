## Formula
**SI = (P × R × T)/100**, and **Amount A = P + SI**.

## Terms
1. **Principal (P):** the money borrowed or invested.
2. **Rate (R):** interest per ₹100 per year (% per annum).
3. **Time (T):** in years. Convert months ÷ 12, days ÷ 365.
4. **Amount (A):** principal plus interest.

## Rearranged formulas
- P = 100 × SI/(R × T)
- R = 100 × SI/(P × T)
- T = 100 × SI/(P × R)
- A = P(1 + RT/100)

## Calculating simple interest
*Example:* ₹5,000 at 8% p.a. for 3 years → SI = 5000 × 8 × 3/100 = **₹1,200**, amount ₹6,200.

## Shortcuts
- A sum becomes **n times** in T years → R = 100(n − 1)/T. It doubles in 8 years → R = 12.5%.
- Amount A₁ after T₁ years and A₂ after T₂ years → SI per year = (A₂ − A₁)/(T₂ − T₁), then P = A₁ − T₁ × (yearly SI).
- SI is the same every year (unlike compound interest).
