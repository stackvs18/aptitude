## What it is
An incomplete sentence or statement; choose the option that completes it logically and grammatically.

## Approaches
- Understand the **tone and logic**: cause–effect, contrast, example or conclusion.
- Look for **signal words**: because/since (reason), but/although/however (contrast), therefore/thus (result), for example (illustration).
- Predict the ending in your own words before reading the options.

## Tips and tricks
- The ending must agree in grammar (tense, number) with the first part.
- Eliminate endings that are true in general but don't connect to the beginning.
- In two-blank questions, check the easier blank first to cut the options down.

## Example
"Although he studied hard, ____." → (a) he passed easily (b) **he could not clear the exam** → (b), because "although" needs a contrast.
