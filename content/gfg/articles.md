## Definite article: "the"
Used when the noun is specific or already known.
- Something mentioned before: "I saw a dog. **The** dog was barking."
- Unique things: **the** sun, **the** moon, **the** earth.
- Superlatives and ordinals: **the** best, **the** first.
- Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, groups of islands: **the** Ganga, **the** Himalayas, **the** Andamans.
- Newspapers, famous buildings and holy books: **the** Hindu, **the** Taj Mahal, **the** Gita.
- Plural country names and "republic/kingdom": **the** USA, **the** Netherlands.
- Musical instruments: play **the** guitar.

## Indefinite articles: "a" and "an"
Used with singular countable nouns that aren't specific.
- **"a"** before a consonant **sound**: a book, **a** university, **a** one-way street, **a** European.
- **"an"** before a vowel **sound**: an apple, **an** hour, **an** honest man, **an** MBA, **an** FIR.
- One of a group: "She is **a** teacher."

## No article
- Names of people, most countries and cities, languages and subjects: "She speaks French."
- Meals, games and general plurals: "We play cricket", "Dogs are loyal".
- Places used for their purpose: go to **school**, go to **church**, in **hospital** (as a patient).

## Definite vs indefinite
"a/an" = any one; "the" = that particular one.
