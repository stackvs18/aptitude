## What it is
Find the **odd one out**: the word (or number) that doesn't belong to the group the others form.

## Types of classification
- **Category:** Rose, Lily, Lotus, **Mango** (fruit among flowers).
- **Function/use:** Pen, Pencil, Marker, **Eraser** (doesn't write).
- **Property:** Iron, Copper, Silver, **Wood** (not a metal).
- **Place/habitat:** Lion, Tiger, Leopard, **Shark**.
- **Letter-based:** BDF, HJL, MOQ, **RTW** (others skip one letter each time).
- **Number-based:** 9, 25, 49, **63**, 81 (others are squares); 3, 5, 7, **9**, 11 (others are prime).

## Tips
- State the group rule in one sentence. If it fits three options exactly, the fourth is the answer.
- Look for the most **specific** common link, not a vague one ("all are things").
