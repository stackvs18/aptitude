## Education and study
- **Bibliophile:** a lover of books. **Pedant:** someone who shows off their learning. **Alumni:** former students. **Polyglot:** someone who speaks many languages.

## Fears (phobias)
- **Acrophobia:** heights. **Claustrophobia:** closed spaces. **Hydrophobia:** water. **Xenophobia:** foreigners. **Nyctophobia:** darkness.

## Conditions and rules
- **Ultimatum:** a final demand. **Amnesty:** a general pardon. **Moratorium:** a temporary ban.

## Groups and collections
- **Anthology:** a collection of poems/stories. **Flotilla:** a fleet of ships. **Bouquet:** a bunch of flowers. **Galaxy:** a group of stars. **Herd:** a group of cattle.

## Likes and dislikes
- **Misanthrope:** hates mankind. **Philanthropist:** loves mankind and helps others. **Misogynist:** hates women. **Epicure:** enjoys fine food.

## Government
- **Autocracy:** rule by one person. **Democracy:** rule by the people. **Plutocracy:** rule by the wealthy. **Anarchy:** absence of government. **Theocracy:** rule by religious leaders.

## People and traits
- **Optimist:** expects the best. **Pessimist:** expects the worst. **Atheist:** doesn't believe in God. **Agnostic:** unsure whether God exists. **Introvert:** inward-looking. **Extrovert:** outgoing. **Novice:** a beginner.

## Professions
- **Cartographer:** makes maps. **Lexicographer:** compiles dictionaries. **Numismatist:** collects coins. **Philatelist:** collects stamps. **Ornithologist:** studies birds. **Cardiologist:** heart specialist. **Dermatologist:** skin specialist.

## Science and research
- **Botany:** study of plants. **Zoology:** study of animals. **Seismology:** study of earthquakes. **Entomology:** study of insects. **Etymology:** study of word origins.

## Killing and death
- **Homicide:** killing a person. **Suicide:** killing oneself. **Regicide:** killing a king. **Genocide:** killing a whole race. **Infanticide:** killing an infant. **Posthumous:** after death.

## Sounds, events and places
- **Cacophony:** harsh sound. **Euphony:** pleasant sound. **Aviary:** a place for birds. **Apiary:** a place for bees. **Arsenal:** a store of weapons. **Granary:** a store of grain.
