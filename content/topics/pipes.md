## ① The idea
Exactly like Time & Work: an **inlet** pipe does positive work, an **outlet**/leak does **negative** work.

## ② Formulas
- Inlet fills in a h, outlet empties in b h (b > a): net filling time = ab/(b − a).
- Pipe fills in a h but with a leak takes t h → leak alone empties in at/(t − a).
- Same LCM units method: tank capacity = LCM; inlet rate +, outlet rate −.

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** A = 20 min, B = 30 min → **12 min**.

**Ex 2.** Tap 8 h, leak 12 h → 8 × 12/(12 − 8) = **24 h**.

**Ex 3.** A 20 h, B 30 h fill; C 40 h empties. LCM 120: +6, +4, −3 = 7 → 120/7 = **17 1/7 h**.

**Ex 4 (leak found).** Pump fills in 2 h; due to a leak takes 2⅓ h → leak = 2 × (7/3)/(7/3 − 2) = (14/3)/(1/3) = **14 h**.

**Ex 5 (time on the clock).** Fill 48 min, empty 120 min, opened 11:40 → net 48 × 120/72 = 80 min → **1:00 PM**.

**Ex 6 (opened then closed).** A fills in 12 min, B in 15 min. Both opened; after 3 min A is closed. Total time? LCM 60: A 5, B 4 → 3 min → 27 units; remaining 33 by B → 8.25 min → total **11 min 15 s**.

**Ex 7 (number of pipes).** 8 pipes: fillers fill in 12 h each, wasters empty in 36 h each; all open → full in 3 h. x/12 − (8 − x)/36 = 1/3 → x = 5 → **3 waste pipes**.

**Ex 8 (partly full).** Fill 8 h, empty 5 h, tank ¾ full, both open → net empty 3/40 per h → (3/4)/(3/40) = **10 h**.
