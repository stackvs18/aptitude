## ① The idea
Profit is shared in proportion to **(capital × time)**.

## ② Formulas
- Same time: profit ratio = capital ratio.
- Different times: ratio = C₁T₁ : C₂T₂ : C₃T₃.
- Working partner gets a salary or a % first; the **rest** is divided in the capital ratio.
- If capital changes midway, add up the pieces: A puts 3000 for 4 months, then 5000 for 8 months → 3000 × 4 + 5000 × 8 = 52000 units.

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** Shankar ₹1,20,000 for 12 months, Aniket ₹1,90,000 joins after 3 months (9 months) → 1440 : 1710 = 16 : 19 → Aniket gets 19/35 of ₹17.5 lakh = **₹9.5 lakh**.

**Ex 2.** A ₹70000 for 36 months, B ₹105000 for 30, C ₹140000 for 24 → 2520 : 3150 : 3360 = **12 : 15 : 16**.

**Ex 3 (one leaves).** A, B, C invest 8000, 4000, 8000; A leaves after 6 months, profit after 8 months → 48 : 32 : 64 = 3 : 2 : 4.

**Ex 4 (working partner).** A (working) ₹5000, B ₹6000, A gets 12.5% for managing. Profit 880 → A: 110 + 5/11 × 770 = 110 + 350 = **₹460**.

**Ex 5 (reverse — find time).** Profits are 5 : 7 : 8 and capitals are 1 : 2 : 3. Since profit ∝ capital × time, time ∝ profit/capital → 5/1 : 7/2 : 8/3 → multiply by 6 → **30 : 21 : 16**.

## ⑤ Trap
"Joined after 3 months" in a 12-month year means **9 months**. "After 2 years" means the total period is 24 months.
