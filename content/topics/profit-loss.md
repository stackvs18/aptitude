## ① The idea
**C.P.** = what you pay. **S.P.** = what you sell for. **M.P.** (marked/list price) = printed price before discount.
**Profit/Loss % is ALWAYS calculated on C.P.** Discount % is ALWAYS on M.P.

## ② Formulas
- Gain = S.P. − C.P.; Gain % = Gain/C.P. × 100.
- S.P. = C.P. × (100 + gain%)/100 or C.P. × (100 − loss%)/100.
- C.P. = S.P. × 100/(100 + gain%) or S.P. × 100/(100 − loss%).
- **Overheads** (repair, transport, tax paid by buyer) are added to C.P.
- **C.P. of x articles = S.P. of y articles** → gain/loss % = (x − y)/y × 100 (gain if x > y).
- **Dishonest dealer** (false weight, sells at C.P.): gain % = error/(true value − error) × 100.
- **Two items sold at the same S.P., one at x% gain, other at x% loss** → always a **loss of x²/100 %**.
- **Discount:** S.P. = M.P. × (100 − d)/100. Successive discounts d₁, d₂ → single = d₁ + d₂ − d₁d₂/100.
- **Marked up m% and discount d%** → gain % = m − d − md/100.
- Profit when selling at S₁ = loss when selling at S₂ → C.P. = (S₁ + S₂)/2.

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** Sold for ₹2850 at 14% gain → C.P. = 2850 × 100/114 = **2500**. For 8% gain: S.P. = **2700**.

**Ex 2.** Bought for ₹4700, ₹800 repairs, sold ₹5800 → C.P. 5500, gain 300 → **5 5/11%**.

**Ex 3.** C.P. of 10 = S.P. of 7 → gain = 3/7 × 100 = **42 6/7 %**.

**Ex 4.** Shopkeeper uses 900 g instead of 1 kg at C.P. → gain = 100/900 × 100 = **11 1/9 %**.

**Ex 5.** Two cycles sold at ₹990 each, one at 10% gain other 10% loss → loss = 1%. (Actual: C.P.s 900 and 1100; total C.P. 2000, S.P. 1980 → loss 20 = 1%.)

**Ex 6.** Mark up 40%, discount 25% → 40 − 25 − 10 = **5% gain**.

**Ex 7.** Selling at ₹1754 profit = loss at ₹1492 → C.P. = **1623**.

**Ex 8.** By selling 45 lemons for ₹40 a man loses 20%. How many for ₹24 to gain 20%? C.P. of 45 = 40/0.8 = 50. Need S.P. = 1.2 × C.P. ₹24 must be 1.2 × C.P. → C.P. = 20 worth → 45 × 20/50 = **18 lemons**.

**Ex 9.** Buys 6 for ₹5, sells 5 for ₹6 → C.P. per item 5/6, S.P. 6/5 → gain = (6/5 − 5/6)/(5/6) = (36 − 25)/25 = **44%**.
Shortcut: gain = (6 × 6 − 5 × 5)/(5 × 5) × 100.

## ⑤ Traps
- "Gains ₹15 by selling at ₹100" → gain % = 15/85, not 15%.
- Discount is on M.P.; profit on C.P. Don't mix bases.
