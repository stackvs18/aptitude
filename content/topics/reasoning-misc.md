## Number series — check in this order
1. **Differences** (and differences of differences): 2, 6, 12, 20, 30 → +4, +6, +8, +10 → next +12 = **42**.
2. **Ratios** (×2, ×3 …) or ×a ± b: 3, 5, 9, 17, 33 → ×2 − 1 → **65**.
3. **Squares/cubes ± k**: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26 = n² + 1.
4. **Alternating** two series: 4, 6, 12, 14, 28, 30 → +2, ×2 → **60**.
5. **Primes**: 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 …

## Letter series
Convert to numbers: A, C, F, J, O → 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 (+2, +3, +4, +5) → next 21 = **U**.

## Odd one out (Odd Man Out)
Look for: prime vs composite, even vs odd, perfect squares/cubes, category (planet vs satellite), one number not following a pattern.
*Ex:* 41, 43, 47, 53, 61, 71, 73, **81** (only non-prime).

## Analogy
Find the relation in the first pair, apply it to the second: Doctor : Hospital (worker : workplace) :: Teacher : **School**. 3 : 27 (cube) :: 4 : **64**.

## Clocks (RSA Ch 28)
- Minute hand moves **6° per minute**; hour hand moves **0.5° per minute** (30° per hour).
- **Angle at H:M = |30H − 5.5M|** (if > 180°, subtract from 360°).
  *Ex:* 3:40 → |90 − 220| = **130°**.
- **Hands coincide** between H and H+1 at **(60/11) × H** = 5 5/11 × H minutes past H. Between 4 and 5: 21 9/11 min past 4.
- Hands are at **right angles** 44 times a day, **coincide** 22 times a day, in a **straight line** (opposite) 22 times a day.
- **Mirror image** of a time = 11:60 − time (for times between 1:00 and 11:00). 4:20 → **7:40**.

## Ranking
- Total = (rank from left) + (rank from right) − 1.
- Rank from bottom = Total − rank from top + 1.

## Mathematical operations (symbol substitution)
Replace each symbol as told, then apply **BODMAS**: 6 − 9 + 8 × 3 ÷ 20 (with + = ×, − = +, × = ÷, ÷ = −) → 6 + 9 × 8 ÷ 3 − 20 = 6 + 24 − 20 = **10**.

## Venn diagrams
- One category completely inside another (Doctors ⊂ Professionals) → circle inside circle.
- Two non-overlapping categories inside a third (Doctors, Lawyers ⊂ Professionals) → two separate circles inside a big circle.
- Partial overlap (Women, Doctors, Mothers) → overlapping circles.
