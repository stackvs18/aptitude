## ① The idea
- **Direct proportion:** more of A → more of B (more articles → more cost; more men → more work done).
- **Inverse proportion:** more of A → less of B (more men → fewer days; more speed → less time).

## ② The master formula
> **(M₁ × D₁ × H₁ × E₁)/W₁ = (M₂ × D₂ × H₂ × E₂)/W₂**
> M = men, D = days, H = hours per day, E = efficiency, W = work (or output, earnings, food eaten).

**Method (unitary):** write the unknown last; for each other quantity ask "if this increases, does the unknown increase (multiply by bigger/smaller) or decrease (smaller/bigger)?"

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** 5.5 dozen pens = ₹1287 → 66 pens → ₹19.50 each → 16 pens = **₹312**.

**Ex 2.** 56 men take 24 days → 42 men take 56 × 24/42 = **32 days** (inverse).

**Ex 3.** 39 men, 12 days, 5 h/day → 30 men, 6 h/day: 39 × 12 × 5 = 30 × D × 6 → **D = 13**.

**Ex 4.** 6 persons, 8 h/day earn ₹8400/week; 9 persons, 6 h/day → 8400 × (9 × 6)/(6 × 8) = **₹9450**.

**Ex 5 (absentees).** Planned 10 days; 5 absent → 12 days. x × 10 = (x − 5) × 12 → **x = 30**.

**Ex 6 (food).** Food for 120 men or 200 children; 150 children eat → 50 children's share left = 50 × 120/200 = **30 men**.

**Ex 7 (food for a garrison).** 500 men have food for 27 days. After 3 days, 300 more men join. Food lasts? Remaining food = 500 × 24 man-days; now 800 men → 500 × 24/800 = **15 days**.

**Ex 8 (gear wheels).** 6-cog wheel makes 21 revolutions → 14-cog wheel makes 6 × 21/14 = **9**.

**Ex 9 (trick).** 7 maids, 7 mops, 7 floors, 7 h → 1 maid does 1 floor in 7 h → 3 maids do 3 floors in **7 h**.
