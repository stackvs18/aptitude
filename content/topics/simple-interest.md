## ① The idea
Interest is paid only on the original principal, the same amount every year.

## ② Formulas
- **S.I. = P × R × T / 100**; Amount A = P + S.I.
- P = 100 × S.I./(R × T); R = 100 × S.I./(P × T); T = 100 × S.I./(P × R).
- Time in months → divide by 12; in days → divide by 365 (count days carefully: exclude the first day, include the last).
- **Sum becomes n times in T years** → R = 100(n − 1)/T.
- Amount after T₁ yrs = A₁ and after T₂ yrs = A₂ → S.I. per year = (A₂ − A₁)/(T₂ − T₁).
- **Present worth** of amount A due in T years = 100A/(100 + RT).

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** ₹5760 at 6% for 3 yrs → 5760 × 18/100 = **₹1036.80**.

**Ex 2.** ₹815 in 3 yrs, ₹854 in 4 yrs → 1 yr interest = 39 → P = 815 − 117 = **₹698**.

**Ex 3.** Sum doubles in 8 yrs → R = 100/8 = **12.5%**. In how many years becomes 4 times? 3P interest → 3 × 8 = **24 yrs**.

**Ex 4.** ₹1600 gives ₹252 in 2 yrs 4 months → T = 7/3 → R = 25200/(1600 × 7/3) = **6.75%**.

**Ex 5.** Present worth of ₹132 due in 2 yrs at 5% → 132 × 100/110 = **₹120**.

**Ex 6 (rate change).** ₹6200 → ₹9176 in 4 yrs. If rate +3%, extra interest = 6200 × 3 × 4/100 = 744 → **₹9920**.

**Ex 7 (split investment).** ₹10000 split into two parts at 6% and 8%; total annual interest ₹720. Use alligation: average rate 7.2% → ratio (8 − 7.2) : (7.2 − 6) = 0.8 : 1.2 = 2 : 3 → **₹4000 at 6%, ₹6000 at 8%**.

## ⑤ Traps
- 2 yrs 3 months = 2.25 yrs, not 2.3.
- "Amount" includes the principal; "interest" doesn't.
