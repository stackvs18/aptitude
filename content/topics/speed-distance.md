## ① The idea
**Distance = Speed × Time.** Everything else is a variation.

## ② Formulas
- Speed = D/T; Time = D/S.
- km/h → m/s: **× 5/18**; m/s → km/h: **× 18/5**.
- **Average speed = total distance / total time** (never the average of the speeds).
  Equal distances at x and y: **2xy/(x + y)**.
- **Constant distance:** speed ratio a : b → time ratio **b : a**.
- If speed becomes (a/b) of usual → time becomes (b/a) of usual → change in time = late/early minutes.
- **Relative speed:** opposite directions → **add**; same direction → **subtract**.
- Two people start at the same time from A and B towards each other, and after meeting take t₁ and t₂ to reach the other ends: S₁ : S₂ = √t₂ : √t₁.
- Late/early problems: d = (S₁ × S₂/(S₂ − S₁)) × (total time difference).

## ③ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** 30.6 km/h = 30.6 × 5/18 = **8.5 m/s**.

**Ex 2.** 60 out, 40 back → 2 × 60 × 40/100 = **48 km/h**.

**Ex 3 (late/early).** At 5 km/h 7 min late, at 6 km/h 5 min early → difference 12 min = 1/5 h → d = (5 × 6/1) × 1/5 = **6 km**.

**Ex 4 (fraction of speed).** At ¾ speed, 20 min late → time × 4/3 → ⅓ of usual = 20 → usual = **60 min**.

**Ex 5 (more distance).** Walks 14 instead of 10 km/h → 20 km more in same time → t = 20/4 = 5 h → actual distance 50 km.

**Ex 6 (meeting).** 300 km apart, 40 and 60 km/h towards each other → **3 h**, meet 120 km from the slower one's start.

**Ex 7 (chase).** A thief runs at 8 km/h; police start 10 min later at 10 km/h. Catch-up time? Lead = 8 × 1/6 = 4/3 km; relative 2 km/h → 2/3 h = **40 min**.

**Ex 8 (halts).** 65 km/h, 1300 km in 24 h, 8 equal halts → running 20 h → 4 h of halts → **30 min each**.

**Ex 9 (stoppage per hour).** Without stops 54 km/h, with stops 45 km/h → stops (54 − 45)/54 × 60 = **10 min per hour**.

## ⑤ Traps
- Mixing km/h with m and s — convert first.
- "Average speed" for unequal times: total D/total T.
