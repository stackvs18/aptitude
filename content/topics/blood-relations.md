## ① The idea
Decode the sentence **from the end backwards**, step by step, and draw a family tree.

## ② Notation for your rough tree
- Male = □ or "+", female = ○ or "−".
- Couple: horizontal double line (A = B). Siblings: horizontal single line. Parent → child: vertical line.
- Generations in rows.

## ③ Must-know relation vocabulary
| Phrase | Means |
|---|---|
| Father's / mother's father | Grandfather |
| Son's / daughter's son | Grandson |
| Father's brother | Uncle (paternal) |
| Mother's brother | Maternal uncle (mama) |
| Father's/mother's sister | Aunt |
| Brother's / sister's son | Nephew |
| Brother's / sister's daughter | Niece |
| Uncle's / aunt's child | Cousin |
| Wife's / husband's father | Father-in-law |
| Wife's brother; sister's husband | Brother-in-law |
| Husband's sister; brother's wife | Sister-in-law |
| Son's wife | Daughter-in-law |
| Daughter's husband | Son-in-law |
| **Only son of my father / my father's only son** | **Myself (if male)** |
| **Only daughter of my mother** | **Myself (if female)** |

## ④ Worked examples
**Ex 1.** "I have no brother or sister, but that man's father is my father's son." → my father's son = me → that man's father = me → **my son**.

**Ex 2.** "His mother is the only daughter of my mother" (said by a woman) → only daughter of my mother = me → **I am his mother**.

**Ex 3.** "His only brother is the father of my daughter's father" (Deepak). My daughter's father = Deepak → his father = Deepak's father → the gentleman is that man's brother → **uncle**.

**Ex 4 (coded).** A + B: A is brother of B; A − B: A is sister of B; A × B: A is father of B. "C is the son of M" = **M × N − C + F** (M is father of N; N is sister of C; C is brother of F → C is male and M's child).
*Method:* go operator by operator; to prove "son" you need both "C is a child of M" and "C is male".

**Ex 5 (puzzle).** A is father of C, but C is not his son → C is a daughter. E is C's daughter; F is A's spouse → E is **F's granddaughter**.

## ⑤ Traps
- Don't assume gender from a name. "A is the child of B" doesn't tell you A's gender; options like "Brother or cousin" exist for this reason.
- "Brother-in-law of my friend's mother" can be father's brother → cousin's father.
